# Reading Kubernetes and Alloy config is a gap in its own right

After Lesson 1 the user said the Kubernetes files and config were very hard to follow, and that several concepts were new. Understanding the platform and reading its YAML are two separate skills, and the second one is currently blocking the first. Future lessons should teach config through small annotated excerpts, never whole files. A dedicated "reading manifests" lesson may be needed before the traces lessons, which lean on `tempo.yml` and the Grafana datasource config.
